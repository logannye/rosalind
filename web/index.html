Scientific meaning stays separate from scheduling

  • Count every eligible observation. Smaller admitted budgets can mean smaller tiles and more indexed I/O. Successful exact-evidence output does not change with budget, tile width or worker count.
  • Know what a number means. Filters, sample scope, fields and counting rules are explicit. An unmeasured locus stays distinct from a stored zero-depth row.
  • Reuse verifiable artifacts. Portable datasets, Arrow/TSV output, Parquet export and Python/R/SQL adapters let consumers build on saved evidence and its lineage.

Understand and verify a result

Inspect receipt integrity in your browser Query and replay saved evidence Use Nextflow or Snakemake

The browser verifier inspects the receipt. Full artifact verification needs the recorded files; byte replay reruns the operation with its dependencies. A receipt is tamper-evident, not proof of authorship or biological accuracy.

Research scope. This profile counts aligned read bases, not molecules; overlapping mates count separately. It is not a clinical interpretation system, UMI consensus engine or validated production caller. Resource admission and sampled checks are cooperative: native decoders can allocate before a checkpoint, including during CRAM validation. Existing Linux cgroup limits provide a separate OS assurance. Representative performance and independent adoption remain explicit validation goals.

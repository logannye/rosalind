# Adoption and delivery tracking

The accepted [delivery roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and its machine-readable
[task inventory](roadmap.json) are the current ordered backlog. Use
[capability and release status](implementation-status.md) for availability and
source-specific validation; do not infer release or adoption from a completed
implementation PR.

The current product sequence is truthful GitHub entry points, executable
researcher/builder onboarding, corrected CRAM validation, an immutable 0.5 RC,
the seven-day soak and technical stable-promotion gates, with local cohort
reanalysis developed separately on the next minor line. Independent persona
feedback is advisory and can continue after publication. Existing exact evidence,
panel QC, cache/resume, physical projection, variant annotation, portable datasets/Parquet and managed SDK work
are foundations, not new backlog items.

Use the [researcher and builder validation kit](adoption-validation.md) to record
real tasks, assistance, failed attempts, first-result time and work avoided.
The analyzer-builder, workflow-HPC and constrained/offline scenarios remain useful
feedback protocols. They and the researcher/builder/workflow sessions are adoption
objectives, not implementation or publication prerequisites. Real return use after
at least 30 days is an adoption measure. Preserve failed attempts and unknown
outcomes rather than treating release eligibility as evidence of user success.

## Acceptance

- Three non-authors complete useful tasks, with failures and assistance recorded.
- One independent analyzer and one external workflow use the evidence interfaces.
- Two teams supply recurring candidate-reanalysis questions and comparison baselines.
- Two teams perform another meaningful task after at least 30days; missing replies
  stay unknown, rather than being counted as successes.
- Reuse comparisons include initial creation/import, storage, hashing, native
  extraction or CRAM validation, verification and subsequent-query costs.
- Published case studies identify exact source/packages, workload and limits and
  contain only consented, reviewed, anonymized material.

No independent sessions, returning teams or commercial outcomes are created by
publishing this backlog or its blank forms. See [maintainer releases](MAINTAINER_RELEASES.md)
for account configuration, actual prerelease publication and stable gates.
